# Angel Talk — A Voice for Every Child

> **Angel Talk** is a gentle, family-first **AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication)** app that helps non-verbal and speech-delayed children say what they need, feel, and think — by tapping pictures that speak out loud. Parents, teachers, and therapists can build the child's whole vocabulary in minutes, even just by **speaking**.

---

## What Angel Talk Does

A child taps a picture → Angel Talk speaks it. Tap several pictures → it builds and speaks a full sentence ("I want water", "I feel tired").

Behind a parent PIN, caregivers get a complete toolkit to build, organise, and personalise the child's board — in **English, Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, Spanish, or French**.

## Key Features

### 🗣️ The Child's Talk Board
- **Picture → Voice:** large, easy picture tiles that speak instantly.
- **Sentence Builder strip:** tap words in order to build a sentence and speak it in one go.
- **Whole Phrases mode (Gestalt Language Processing):** one tap speaks a whole sentence such as *"I want pizza, please"*. You can switch between this and Sentence Builder at any time.
- **"Say It For Me" board:** ready-made everyday sentences.
- **Quick Access bar:** Help · Water · Bathroom · Stop, always one tap away.
- **Ready-made shelves:** People, Feelings, Actions, Food, Places, Things, Colours, School, Sports, Hygiene, Music, Vehicles, and more.
- **Natural voices:** boy, girl, woman, or man, with adjustable pitch and speed.

### ✨ Smart Verb Teaching (1st–4th Forms)
- Add a verb once and Angel Talk creates **all four forms** automatically: *Sing → Sang → Sung → Singing*, *Go → Went → Gone → Going*.
- Each tile shows a colour-coded **form badge** (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th).
- **Verbs A–Z shelves:** every verb sits under its own letter, in the right order.
- Edit one form and the others update with it, sharing the same picture.

### 🎙️ Build the Board by Voice
- **Voice Add:** say *"category fruits, sub category citrus, words orange and lemon, sub category berries, words strawberry and grapes"* and Angel Talk builds the whole structure at once: shelves, sub-categories, and words.
- **Understands natural speech:** filler words like "create a… the name of…" are removed, so *"create category the name of apple"* simply becomes **Apple**.
- **Tolerates common speech-recognition mistakes**, such as hearing "category" as "catgires" or "sub category" as "sub period".
- **Review before saving:** every spoken item appears in an editable list. Fix a name, remove a line, or add a missing one. Nothing is created until you confirm.
- **Pictures assigned automatically** for every item, which you can replace with your own.
- Works with Arabic and Urdu voice commands as well.

### 🧰 Caregiver Space (Vocabulary Manager)
- Add words one by one, by **bulk paste** ("book, pen, car"), or by voice.
- **Shelves and sub-categories** (e.g., Food → Fruits, Drinks, Snacks).
- Choose **pictures from:** a built-in library of about 14,800 ARASAAC symbols, the phone gallery, the camera, online image search, or AI image generation.
- **Your choices stay:** a picture you choose stays until you change it, and a word you delete stays deleted.
- Hide or show any word or shelf from the child's board without deleting it.
- Track **use count** and **last used** for every word.
- Back up and restore the whole board.

### 🌍 Truly Multilingual
- Full app and board in **English, العربية (Arabic), اردو (Urdu), हिन्दी (Hindi), Español, and Français**.
- **Dual-language tiles:** show English plus Arabic or Urdu on the same tile.
- **The same layout in every language:** switching language changes only the words, not the screen layout.
- Built-in Arabic and Urdu translations for the starter vocabulary, including verbs.

### 📖 Learning, Routine & Calm Tools
- **Visual Social Stories:** illustrated, read-aloud stories for real-life moments such as Dentist, Haircut, First Day of School, Calming Down, Sharing, Doctor Visit, Supermarket, and Bedtime. Parents can also write their own.
- **Visual Schedule:** a picture-based daily routine (Brush Teeth → Breakfast → School → Play → Dinner → Sleep).
- **Learning Games:** Letters, Numbers, Colours, Shapes, Animals, Food, and Emotions.
- **Sensory Calmer:** guided breathing (breathe in, hold, breathe out) for overwhelming moments.
- **Emergency Passcard:** key information about the child for caregivers, teachers, and emergency staff.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent & Therapist Tools
- **90-second onboarding:** tell Angel Talk your child's favourite foods, family members, and toys, and it builds **"⭐ [Child's Name]'s World"** automatically.
- **Parent Dashboard:** therapy goals, a daily care and meltdown journal, and caregiver notes.
- **Content Review Queue:** approve pictures before they reach the child.
- **Content filter** for anything added to the board.
- **PIN-protected adult area,** so the child sees only their board.

### 🔒 Private by Design
- No forced account and no subscription lock-in.
- The board and vocabulary are stored **on the device**.
- The core talk board works **offline**. Voice building and online picture search use the internet only when you use them.

## How It Works

1. **Set up in about 90 seconds:** choose the language and voice, and add your child's favourite things.
2. **Build the board:** use the ready-made shelves, or add your own words by typing, pasting, or speaking.
3. **The child talks:** they tap pictures, build sentences, and are heard.
